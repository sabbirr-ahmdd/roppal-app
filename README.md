## About Roppal

Roppal is a SaaS retention tool built by Orviton. The goal is simple:
help SaaS founders and teams spot at-risk customers before they
cancel, not after.

Instead of relying on billing failures to signal churn, Roppal watches
customer logins, payment activity, and product usage every day,
building a health score per customer so you know who's disengaging
while there's still time to act.

## What Roppal Does

- **Daily Health Score:** combines login activity, payment status, and
  usage to score every customer's churn risk, automatically
- **Automatic Outreach:** when a score drops, Roppal sends a
  personalized email to that customer, no approval needed
- **Follow-up Check-in:** if there's no response, Roppal sends one
  short follow-up asking why, and routes the reply straight to your inbox
- **Human Handoff:** if a customer stays silent, Roppal alerts your
  team directly so a real person can step in
